Asthma and Air Quality in the Bronx

Do New York City neighborhoods with more fine-particle air pollution (PM2.5) have higher rates of asthma emergency department visits, and does that link hold after accounting for neighborhood poverty? The Bronx has the city's highest child asthma hospitalization rate, so I highlight its neighborhoods within a citywide comparison.

Data
  • Asthma ED visits per 10,000 residents, by community district
  • Annual PM2.5 (µg/m³) and NO2 (ppb) from the NYC Community Air Survey
  • Neighborhood poverty rate

Source: NYC Environment & Health Data Portal

Methods
  • Merge ~59 community districts into one dataset
  • Scatter plots and a neighborhood map
  • Correlation and multiple regression (PM2.5 + poverty)
  • Residual analysis for Bronx districts

Tools: Python (pandas, matplotlib), Excel
